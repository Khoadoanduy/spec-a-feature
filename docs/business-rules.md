### BR-NOT-01: Definition of Non-Submitter
A student is classified as a non-submitter for a specific reporting artifact (Weekly Activity Report or Peer Evaluation) in an active week if and only if:
1. The student is currently assigned to an active team in the course section (BR-team-assignment-required).
2. The current week is configured as an active week for the course section (BR-active-weeks).
3. The student has no valid, non-deleted submission record for that artifact for the target week.

### BR-NOT-02: Targeted Nudge Window & Rate Limits
1. Targeted reminder notifications (nudges) may only be sent for a reporting period while its submission window remains open.
2. An instructor may manually trigger a nudge to the same student at most once per 24-hour period per active submission artifact to prevent email flooding.
